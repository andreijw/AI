# GenericWorld-v0 Multi-Agent Benchmark Results

Comparative benchmark evaluation across reinforcement learning algorithms.

## Benchmark Configuration

- **Challenge / Environment**: `GenericWorld-v0` (Solved criteria: average reward >= 20)
- **Budget per Run**: each agent's own config (`training.total_steps` or `num_episodes`), shown per agent
- **Seeds Evaluated**: `[1, 2]`
- **Algorithms**: `['long', 'short']`

## How a Run Counts as Solved

A run is solved when its average training reward over 1 consecutive
episodes reaches 20. **Avg Solve Episode** is the last episode of
the first such window, and **Avg Steps to Solve** the training steps up to it,
averaged over the solved runs. **Held** is the share of runs with at least
1 episodes whose last 1 average at least 20.
**Eval ≥ Threshold** is the share of runs whose final evaluation
(1 greedy episode) averaged at least 20; it does not count as a solve.
